# NimbusDrive Help Center (fictional product, used as the bot's knowledge base)

## Plans and pricing
- Free: 5 GB storage, max file size 1 GB.
- Plus: 200 GB storage, $2.99/month, max file size 10 GB.
- Pro: 2 TB storage, $9.99/month, max file size 50 GB, priority support.
- Annual billing gives 2 months free.

## Refunds
- Refunds are available within 14 days of your first purchase of a paid plan.
- Renewals are not refundable. Cancel before the renewal date to avoid being charged.

## Account and security
- Reset password: Sign-in page > "Forgot password" > enter your email > open the link (valid 30 minutes).
- Two-factor authentication (2FA): Settings > Security > Enable 2FA. Authenticator apps and SMS are supported.
- Files are encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.

## Files and sharing
- Deleted files stay in Trash for 30 days and can be restored from Trash > Restore.
- Share a file: right-click > Share > Copy link. Links can be set to view-only or editable, and can expire (Plus and Pro only).
- Desktop apps are available for Windows and macOS. Mobile apps for iOS and Android.

## Support
- Email: support@nimbusdrive.example
- Hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00-18:00 UTC. Pro users get replies within 4 hours.
